About SET Days

SET Days 2026 is a proposed three-day event of the School of Engineering and Technology (SET) at National University – Fairview, scheduled for December 2–4, 2026. Under the theme “Learn. Build. Innovate,” it connects classroom topics in software, networking, and hardware with hands-on practice.

The purpose of the event is to strengthen technical skills, teamwork, and problem-solving. Through workshops, timed competitions, and project demonstrations, students apply what they have learned and receive feedback from faculty and peers.

SET Days is intended for NU Fairview students in Information Technology, Computer Science, and Engineering programs, together with SET faculty members and student organizations. Participation steps are listed under Participation Guidelines.

Proposed Activities

Six proposed workshops, competitions, and exhibitions across the three days.

  • Day 1 · Workshop

    AI and Machine Learning Workshop

    An introductory hands-on session covering dataset preparation and supervised learning models.

  • Day 1 · Bootcamp

    Web Development Bootcamp

    A practical coding workshop on semantic HTML5, responsive CSS3 layouts, and accessibility.

  • Day 2 · Competition

    Cybersecurity Challenge

    A beginner-friendly Capture-the-Flag (CTF) exercise on web security fundamentals and log analysis.

  • Day 2 · Competition

    Programming Competition

    A timed problem-solving contest testing algorithms, data structures, and coding accuracy.

  • Day 3 · Demonstration

    Robotics Demonstration

    A live hardware showcase of microcontroller prototypes, sensor systems, and automated robots.

  • Day 3 · Showcase

    Student Innovation Showcase

    A campus exhibit of software, mobile, and hardware capstone projects by SET students.

How to Join an Activity

  1. Pick an activity above and note its day and room in the schedule.
  2. Check whether it is an individual workshop or a team competition of two to three students.
  3. Bring a laptop and your school ID for laboratory sessions.
  4. Arrive at the assigned room 15 minutes before the start time.

Key Terms

Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML)
Algorithms and statistical models that let systems recognize patterns and make data-driven predictions.
Capture the Flag (CTF)
A cybersecurity exercise where participants solve security tasks to uncover hidden validation flags.
Semantic HTML5
Standard elements such as <header>, <nav>, <section>, and <footer> that describe the meaning and structure of a page.
